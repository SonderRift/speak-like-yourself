Is Brazilian Portuguese Hard to Learn? An Honest Answer

Is Brazilian Portuguese hard to learn? An honest answer from a Brazilian teacher: the easy parts, the three genuinely tricky bits, and how long it takes.

Rafael · Brazilian Portuguese teacherAugust 11, 2026 · 2 min read

Is Brazilian Portuguese hard to learn? Short answer: For an English speaker, no, it is one of the easier languages to start speaking. But that does not mean it is effortless. As a teacher, I would rather give you the honest version: the parts that are surprisingly easy, the three things that genuinely trip people up, and what actually determines whether you succeed.

Why it is easier than you fear

Portuguese shares a huge amount of vocabulary with English through Latin roots, so words like importante, possível and natural are basically free. Spelling is phonetic: once you learn the rules you can read almost any word correctly, unlike English. And the US Foreign Service Institute ranks Portuguese in its easiest group for English speakers, around 600 to 750 hours to working proficiency. If you have ever studied Spanish, you are even further ahead. Start with the beginner roadmap and you will feel progress in weeks.

The three things that actually trip people up

Honesty time. Three parts take real practice:

  1. Nasal vowels (ão, ãe, õe): sounds English does not have, so your ear builds them from scratch.
  2. Two verbs for “to be” (ser and estar): permanent versus temporary, a distinction English packs into one word.
  3. Verb conjugations: more endings to learn than English, though patterns repeat quickly.

None of these is a wall. They are just the parts worth slowing down for.

What really decides if you succeed

Here is the truth after years of teaching: the difficulty of the language matters far less than your consistency. The learner who does 20 honest minutes a day beats the one who studies hard for a week and quits. Brazilian Portuguese is not hard. Stopping is what makes any language hard. Pick a sustainable routine, lean on the self-study system, and the language takes care of itself.

The takeaway

Is Brazilian Portuguese hard? Not really. It is friendlier to English speakers than most languages, with a few spots worth patience. What decides your success is showing up daily. Grab the free phrases below and prove it to yourself this week.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brazilian Portuguese harder than Spanish?

They are similar in difficulty for English speakers. Portuguese has trickier vowel sounds; Spanish pronunciation is a touch simpler. If you know one, the other comes fast.

How long until I can hold a conversation?

With 30 minutes a day, simple conversations in 3 to 6 months is realistic. See the [beginner roadmap](/speak-like-yourself/blog/how-to-learn-brazilian-portuguese/) for the order that gets you there fastest.

What is the hardest part of Brazilian Portuguese?

For most English speakers, the nasal vowels and the two "to be" verbs. Both are very learnable with a little focused practice.
