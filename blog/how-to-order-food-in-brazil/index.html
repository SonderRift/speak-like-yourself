How to Order Food in Brazil: Restaurant Portuguese

Restaurant Portuguese phrases for Brazil: how to get a table, order food and drinks, handle dietary needs, and pay, with real phrases and cultural tips.

Rafael · Brazilian Portuguese teacherAugust 11, 2026 · 3 min read

Eating out is one of the best parts of Brazil, and a few restaurant Portuguese phrases turn a stressful order into a warm exchange. Waiters are patient, portions are generous, and trying Portuguese gets you extra smiles. Here is everything you need, from getting a table to asking for the check, plus a couple of cultural tips that catch tourists off guard.

Getting a table and the menu

Portuguese English
Uma mesa para dois, por favor A table for two, please
O cardápio, por favor The menu, please
Você tem menu em inglês? Do you have a menu in English?
O que você recomenda? What do you recommend?
Qual é o prato do dia? What’s the dish of the day?

Ordering food and drinks

The magic word is eu gostaria de (I would like), softer and more polite than eu quero (I want).

Portuguese English
Eu gostaria… I would like…
Para mim, … For me, …
Uma água (sem gás / com gás) A water (still / sparkling)
Uma cerveja bem gelada A nice cold beer
Um cafezinho, por favor A little coffee, please
Está delicioso! It’s delicious!
Mais um(a), por favor One more, please

Dietary needs and allergies

Important ones. Brazilians are used to accommodating, but be clear.

Portuguese English
Tem opção vegana / vegetariana? Is there a vegan / vegetarian option?
Eu sou vegetariano / vegetariana I am vegetarian (man / woman)
Eu sou vegano / vegana I am vegan
Eu tenho alergia a… I am allergic to…
Eu tenho intolerância ao glúten / à lactose I am gluten / lactose intolerant
Sem glúten, por favor Gluten-free, please
Sem cebola / sem pimenta No onion / not spicy
Isso tem carne? Does this have meat?

Paying the bill

Two things surprise tourists. First, you usually have to ask for the check, it will not just arrive. Second, a couvert (a small charge for bread or appetizers) and a 10-20 percent taxa de serviço (service charge) are often already on the bill, so extra tipping is optional.

Portuguese English
A conta, por favor The check, please
Aceita cartão? Do you take card?
O serviço está incluído? Is the service charge included?
Pode dividir a conta? Can you split the bill?
Está certo, obrigado It’s all good, thank you (keep the change)

The takeaway

With a handful of restaurant phrases you can order confidently, handle allergies, and pay without confusion, and Brazilians will love that you tried. Frequently asked questions

Do you tip in restaurants in Brazil?

A 10 percent service charge (taxa de serviço) is usually already on the bill, so extra tipping is optional. You can round up or leave a bit more for great service.

How do I ask for the bill in Brazil?

Say a conta, por favor. The check rarely comes on its own, so you need to ask. Add aceita cartão? to check they take card.

How do I say I'm vegetarian in Portuguese?

Eu sou vegetariano (man) or eu sou vegetariana (woman). For vegan, use vegano/vegana. Get more with the [travel phrases guide](/speak-like-yourself/blog/brazilian-portuguese-phrases-for-travelers/).
