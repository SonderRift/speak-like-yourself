Beleza, Legal, Massa: How Brazilians Really Say 'Cool'

Beleza, legal, massa: what these Brazilian words really mean and how to use them. The many ways Brazilians say 'cool', explained by a native teacher.

Rafael · Brazilian Portuguese teacherAugust 11, 2026 · 2 min read

If you searched for beleza meaning portuguese, you’re in the right place. If you learn how Brazilians say “cool,” you unlock half of casual conversation. The beleza meaning in Portuguese goes way beyond “beauty,” and legal and massa are everywhere. These little words are the flavor of everyday Brazilian speech. Here is what each one really means, when to use it, and how the choice quietly signals where a Brazilian is from.

Beleza: much more than ‘beauty’

Literally beleza means “beauty,” but in daily life it means cool, alright, deal, or all good. As a greeting, beleza? means “you good?” As an answer, beleza! means “cool, that works.” It is one of the most useful words you can own. Reply beleza to a plan, a price, or a “see you at eight” and you sound instantly natural.

Legal: the everyday ‘cool’

Legal (say it “leh-GAOW”) is the workhorse. It means cool, nice, great for basically anything: a place, a person, an idea, a movie. Que legal! is “how cool!” It is neutral, friendly and works in almost any company. If you only learn one, learn this.

Massa and the regional flavors of ‘cool’

Massa (literally “dough/mass”) means awesome, and it is especially loved in the Northeast and among younger people. Brazil has a whole map of “cool”:

Portuguese English
legal cool / nice (everywhere)
massa awesome (strong in the Northeast)
maneiro cool (very Rio)
da hora cool (very São Paulo)
show / show de bola great / awesome
irado sick / awesome
top top-notch / great

How to actually use them

Sprinkle, do not force. Start by replacing your English “cool/nice/great” with legal and answering beleza to plans. Add massa or the regional words once your ear has heard them in the wild. These sit right next to the rest of the Brazilian slang that makes you sound like a local, and pair beautifully with the warm words in saudade and endearment.

The takeaway

Beleza, legal and massa are tiny words that make you sound Brazilian fast. Start with legal for everything and beleza for agreement, then add the regional flavors. The Survival Kit below has the real expressions organized so they stick.

Frequently asked questions

What does beleza mean in Portuguese?

Literally "beauty," but in everyday Brazilian speech it means cool, alright, or deal. Beleza? asks "you good?" and beleza! answers "cool, that works."

What's the difference between legal and massa?

Legal is the neutral, everywhere word for "cool." Massa means "awesome" and is especially common in the Northeast and among younger speakers. Both are friendly and safe.

How do Brazilians say something is cool?

Most often legal or que legal! Also massa, maneiro (Rio), da hora (São Paulo), show and top. More in the [slang guide](/speak-like-yourself/blog/brazilian-portuguese-slang/).
