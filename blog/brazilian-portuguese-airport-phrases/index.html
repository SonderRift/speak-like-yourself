Survival & Travel Phrases

Brazilian Portuguese for the Airport & Getting Around

Portuguese travel phrases for the airport and getting around Brazil: immigration, taxis and apps, buses and directions, with real phrases you can use today.

Rafael · Brazilian Portuguese teacherAugust 11, 2026 · 2 min read

The airport and the first taxi are where travel nerves peak, so these Portuguese travel phrases are built for exactly those moments. From immigration to grabbing a ride to finding your way around, here is the practical language to move through Brazil with confidence, even if it is your first time and your Portuguese is brand new.

At the airport and immigration

Portuguese English
Onde fica o portão de embarque…? Where is the boarding gate…?
Onde fica a esteira de bagagem? Where is the baggage claim?
Estou aqui a turismo I’m here for tourism
Vou ficar duas semanas I’m staying two weeks
Perdi minha conexão I missed my connection
Minha mala não chegou My bag didn’t arrive

Taxis and ride apps

Apps like Uber and 99 are widely used and take the language pressure off, since the address is typed in. For a street taxi, these help. Always agree the ride is on the meter (no taxímetro) or by app price.

Portuguese English
Pode me levar para este endereço? Can you take me to this address?
Quanto custa até o centro? How much to downtown?
É no taxímetro? Is it on the meter?
Pode parar aqui, por favor You can stop here, please

Buses, metro and directions

Portuguese English
Qual ônibus vai para…? Which bus goes to…?
Onde fica a estação de metrô? Where is the metro station?
Este ônibus passa no centro? Does this bus go downtown?
Onde eu compro a passagem? Where do I buy the ticket?
Vira à direita / à esquerda Turn right / left
É longe daqui? Is it far from here?
Estou perdido / perdida I’m lost (man / woman)

One tip that saves you

Screenshot your hotel address and show it if words fail, and learn onde fica…? (where is…?) cold, because you will use it constantly. Pair it with pode repetir mais devagar? (can you repeat more slowly?) and you can navigate almost anything. Once you are settled, the restaurant phrases get you fed.

The takeaway

The airport and the first ride are the scariest ten minutes of any trip, and a few phrases make them smooth. Frequently asked questions

Do I need Portuguese to get around Brazil?

English is limited outside big tourist areas, so a few phrases go a long way. Ride apps help a lot, and onde fica...? plus your hotel address written down covers most situations.

Are taxis or ride apps better in Brazil?

Ride apps (Uber, 99) are popular, cashless and take the language stress off since you type the address. Street taxis are fine too, just confirm the meter (no taxímetro).

How do I ask for directions in Portuguese?

Onde fica...? (where is...?) is the key phrase, plus à direita (right) and à esquerda (left). More in the [travel phrases guide](/speak-like-yourself/blog/brazilian-portuguese-phrases-for-travelers/).
